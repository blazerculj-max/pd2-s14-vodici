Cilj 200 % FCR in 86 % FHR (48 je dovolj).
Merc
Day 1: tank s safety craft helmom in oklepom ter blood crafti. Endgame: Act 3 (Static Field ob vsakem teleportu) s Skyfall, Innocence (decrepify), Occultist in Marrowwalk.

Stati

Oprema
Budget: Demon's Arch (Balrog Spear ima najboljšo min škodo; kasneje crafted throwing orožje), Deception v barb čeladi s +Split Throw (crit chance in multiplier), Hustle (hitrost, breakpointi, half freeze), Razortail (pierce), Steelrend (crushing blow zdaj velja za vse projektile) ali Laying of Hands za IAS, Goblin Toe (crushing blow), crafted prstan s half freeze (Diamond + Hel + jewel; z Hustle CBF) in dual leech prstan, Hit Power craft amulet (Thul + Sapphire + jewel) z resi, pierceom in barb skilli, Head Hunter's Glory (resi, Amplify Damage, socketi), swap Naj's Puzzler (teleport). Max IAS breakpoint, FHR 27/48 (bolje 86). Charmi: max damage za bosse, mastery ali combat skillerji (min škoda) za mape.
Merc
Budget: Act 5 Might z The Vile Husk (Amplify Damage), Harlequin Crest (+skilli za Might, PDR), Guardian Angel, String of Ears, Lava Gout (half freeze, Enchant) in škornji s half freeze. Endgame: Act 1 Vigor s Faith ali Act 5 z Beast.
