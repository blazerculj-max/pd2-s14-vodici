Season 2:
Charge-up skills reworked
Instead of charges being generated one-at-a-time per charge-up attack and the effects only applying when consumed by a finishing move or normal attack:
Charge-up attacks immediately apply the attacks' effect of the appropriate stage (1-3) and generate charges until the character has that many charges of the attacks' type. Charges are "shared" between charge-up attacks so the first attack will apply a stage 1 effect and generate 1 charge, the second attack will apply a stage 2 effect and generate 1-2 charges, and the third attack will apply a stage 3 effect before the charges are reset and the cycle repeats.
Since charges are reset after the 3rd attack rather than before the first attack of the next cycle, it's impossible to have 3 charges of any single type; to get 3 charges, they must be of different types. A finishing move can be used at any point to consume the charges as usual.

Season 2:
Charge-up skills reworked
Instead of charges being generated one-at-a-time per charge-up attack and the effects only applying when consumed by a finishing move or normal attack:
Charge-up attacks immediately apply the attacks' effect of the appropriate stage (1-3) and generate charges until the character has that many charges of the attacks' type. Charges are "shared" between charge-up attacks so the first attack will apply a stage 1 effect and generate 1 charge, the second attack will apply a stage 2 effect and generate 1-2 charges, and the third attack will apply a stage 3 effect before the charges are reset and the cycle repeats.
Since charges are reset after the 3rd attack rather than before the first attack of the next cycle, it's impossible to have 3 charges of any single type; to get 3 charges, they must be of different types. A finishing move can be used at any point to consume the charges as usual.

Blade Sentinel is classified as a summoned creature. It travels back and forth between the point it was cast from and the target location. It travels 24 Yards per second and deals damage for 2 seconds, although startup and death animations make it appear to last longer. Its collision radius is 2 Yards. 
When Blade Sentinel changes direction, it explodes, doing 75% weapon damage in the explosion radius as long as it has been 0.48 Seconds (12 frames) since the last explosion. There is no animation for this explosion. Blade Sentinel's explosion benefits fully from all forms of Resistance Pierce -% at full effect, but does not deal Blade Sentinel's skill damage or receive the flat Attack Rating (it will receive the Attack Rating +%).

Blade Fury uses your Main Hand weapon to perform a ranged attack. If dual wielding, normal rules regarding attack speed and affixes apply. Blades are normal projectiles, requiring Pierce Chance to pierce enemies. 
Blades last 1.12 seconds (28 frames). Each Blade splits into 3 Blades when it hits a wall or other terrain (100% chance to split), or an enemy (50% chance), 3 splits maximum. Every time a blade splits it loses 0.08 seconds (2 frames) from its remaining duration.
Life and Mana Leech work at 75% effectiveness on all Blades; Chance to Cast effects are reduced by 80% on split Blades.
Blade Fury Receives Bonuses From:
Blade Sentinel: +10% Damage per Level

Blade Shield in Project Diablo 2 is a Strike, but not an Attack. This means Blade Shield does not use weapon durability, and "on Striking" but not "on Attack" Chance to Cast effects will function. Blade Shield checks Attack Rating/Defense when Striking an enemy, but Blade Shield from Innocence does not (it will always hit).
