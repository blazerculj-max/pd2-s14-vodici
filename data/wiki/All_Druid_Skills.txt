Poison Creepers do not Attack; they emerge from the ground under their target, creating a vine patch that spreads outward.
Each vine patch "growth" spawns two more vine patches in a random outward direction from the center patch 0.36 seconds (9 frames) after the previous patch.
Vine patches have a 2 yard radius, are functionally identical to poison clouds, and last 4 seconds.
Creepers cannot be targeted or attacked, but can be damaged and killed (by projectiles and AoE damage) while above ground.
Poison Creeper can break Poison Immunity. See Reducing Resistances for more information.
Poison Creeper benefits from neither Poison Resistance Pierce -% (like most summoned creatures), nor +% Poison Skill Damage/Mastery (unlike most other summoned creatures).
